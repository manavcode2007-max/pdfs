## 2. Main Objective

Do not write a generic report about DDoS attacks. The assigned analysis dimension must form the core of the report and account for approximately 40% of its content.

Investigate the following:

- Background of Dyn and the DNS services affected.
- The Mirai botnet's role in the October 2016 attack.

- How Dyn and relevant infrastructure providers responded to the incident.

The core analysis must deeply examine containment and recovery, including:

- Initial mitigation and attack-traffic handling.
